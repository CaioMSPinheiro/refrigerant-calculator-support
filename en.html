Privacy policy

2026-10-28 · Refrigerant Calculator 2.0.0

No data collection by the app

Refrigerant Calculator requires no account and does not send personal data, calculations, history or favorites to developer servers. Calculations run on your device. The app has no integrated paid advertising, tracking or third-party usage analytics services.

Local storage

Preferences, settings, history and favorites are stored on the device. They may include fluid, units, inputs, results and dates. Records remain until you delete them in the app or remove its data. Operating system backups and restores may include this data according to your settings.

Reports and external links

PDF reports are generated locally. They are sent to another app or recipient only when you choose to share them. App Store links and external references open services governed by their own privacy policies. AI Support is disabled in this version.

Support contact

If you contact us by email, your address and the content you send are used to respond to your request. Do not send unnecessary sensitive information. To request deletion of support messages, contact the same email address.

Children and updates

The app is a technical tool and does not collect personal data from children. Changes to this policy will be published on this page with an updated date.
